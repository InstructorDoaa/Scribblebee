      About your teacher

      Meet Ms. Doaa

      Ms. Doaa Mahmoud is an English and early years educator with 17 years in schools. She began her career as an English teacher and an Early Years homeroom teacher, and grew into academic coordination, academic direction and stage leadership across British and international curricula.

      She has led whole stages from Early Years to Year 8, coached teachers through classroom observation and feedback, reviewed curriculum and assessment, and worked closely with parents to support every child's progress.

      Her passion is the foundations: helping young children fall in love with books, hear the sounds in words, and write their first sentences with confidence. Scribblebee brings that work home, so families can follow each week's learning.
